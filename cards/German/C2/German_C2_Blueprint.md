# German (Standard High German) — C2 Sentence-Based Curriculum Blueprint

## 1. Total Scope Assessment

C2 targets **16,000+ vocabulary items**, but at this level sentence-based drilling exists almost entirely to refine control over register, subtext, and stylistic nuance rather than to teach new grammar. Estimated requirement:

**≈ 835 sentences**, divided into 14 topic batches — the smallest total of any level, reflecting that C2 is about precision and range within known structures rather than acquiring new ones.

## 3. Linguistic Justification

- **Register-spectrum control**: C2 speakers must move fluidly between bureaucratic, academic, colloquial, and literary registers within the same conversation — a skill of range rather than new structure (Batch 1).
- **Rhetorical figures**: classical devices like chiasmus and anaphora depend on deliberately manipulating the word-order rules mastered at earlier levels for stylistic effect (Batch 4).
- **Implicature without markers**: irony and subtext at this level often carry no lexical signal (no `ja`, no exclamation), relying entirely on context and prosody — the hardest skill to encode in isolated written sentences (Batch 7).
- **Word-formation productivity**: near-native speakers coin plausible new compounds on the fly following productive but unwritten rules; C2 sentence work should include a sample of these to build recognition (Batch 12).
- **Self-correction and ellipsis**: authentic native spontaneous speech is intentionally imperfect (false starts, ellipsis, resumption) — the only place in this curriculum where "flawed" sentences are pedagogically correct (Batch 14).
