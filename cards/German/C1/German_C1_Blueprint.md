# German (Standard High German) — C1 Sentence-Based Curriculum Blueprint

## 1. Total Scope Assessment

C1 targets roughly **8,000 vocabulary items**, most of it now receptive/passive rather than requiring active drilling. The learning priority shifts toward idiom, register command, and text-type-specific conventions (legal, journalistic, academic, literary). Estimated requirement:

**≈ 1,000 sentences**, divided into 16 topic batches — fewer batches than B2 despite the larger vocabulary, because at this level breadth of exposure matters more than exhaustive per-word drilling.

## 3. Linguistic Justification

- **Free indirect speech (erlebte Rede)**: blends narrator and character voice without explicit marking, requiring tense/pronoun cues alone to signal perspective shift — a literary-register skill absent from earlier levels (Batch 4).
- **Nominal chains in journalism**: headlines and compressed reporting stack multiple genitive/compound nouns without connecting verbs, demanding rapid parsing (Batch 5).
- **Legal passive stacking**: bureaucratic German layers multiple passive and modal-passive constructions in a single sentence, a register unlikely encountered before C1 (Batch 6).
- **Modal/epistemic particles**: precise control of hedging (`vermutlich`, `womöglich`, `dürfte wohl`) distinguishes near-native from merely fluent speech (Batch 3).
- **Ellipsis and inversion in literary/poetic style**: deliberate deviation from standard word order for stylistic effect requires first mastering the standard rules being broken (Batch 13).
