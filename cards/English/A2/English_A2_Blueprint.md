# English (British RP) — A2 Blueprint

## 1. Total Scope Assessment

The A2 band adds approximately **750–1,000 new headwords** (cumulative vocabulary ≈ 1,300–1,750) and introduces a much wider tense/aspect range (past continuous, present perfect, two future forms) plus the first conditional structures and modal system. Because tense-aspect distinctions are the primary new cognitive load at this level, sentence count per grammar point is increased slightly over A1.

- **24 batches**
- **35 sentences per batch**
- **Total: 840 sentences**

## 3. Linguistic Justification

A2 is where English's **aspect system** (simple vs continuous vs perfect) becomes the dominant trap, since many languages either lack a perfect aspect entirely or mark it very differently. Batches 8–9 isolate present perfect specifically against past simple, because learners consistently misuse *since/for* and overextend past simple into "unfinished time" contexts.

**Modal verb defectiveness** — English modals (*can, must, should*) do not inflect, take no *-s* in third person, and require bare infinitives — is another non-obvious trap addressed across batches 10–12, kept separate by function (obligation/advice/permission) so learners don't conflate meanings.

The **first vs zero conditional distinction** (batches 16–17) is a frequent error source because both use present tense in the *if*-clause; only the result clause differs (*will* vs present simple), a subtle formal cue that needs isolated repetition.

For RP specifically, this level introduces more **weak-form auxiliaries** (*have*, *will*, *would*) and **linking-r** across word boundaries (*far away*, *here is*) — sentence content is chosen so these connected-speech features recur naturally within grammar-focused batches, reinforcing accent alongside structure.
