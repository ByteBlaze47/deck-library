# English (British RP) — C2 Blueprint

## 1. Total Scope Assessment

C2 targets **near-native mastery**, with an effective receptive/productive vocabulary ceiling of **16,000+ headwords** (including archaic, technical, regional, and literary lexis) and full command of every register, rhetorical device, and prosodic feature of RP. At this level there are no new grammatical rules to learn — the work is entirely about **precision, nuance, and stylistic control** across registers, which requires the widest sentence exposure of any level.

- **40 batches**
- **55 sentences per batch**
- **Total: 2,200 sentences**

## 3. Linguistic Justification

At C2, grammatical error is essentially eliminated as a variable, so the structural "traps" shift entirely to **register control and prosody**. Batches 3–7 isolate five distinct registers precisely because English (uniquely among the languages many learners come from) allows radically different syntax for the same proposition depending on register — legal English retains archaic structures (*"the said party"*, *shall*) that have disappeared from every other register, while journalistic English favours front-loaded noun clusters (*"government minister and former ambassador John Smith..."*) that would be ungrammatical in conversation.

**Extraposition and complex ellipsis** (batches 14–15) are isolated because native speakers routinely omit or displace elements that non-native speakers over-specify, and mastering *when* to omit is as important as mastering *how* to include.

Because the target is specifically **RP**, batch 39 is dedicated entirely to prosody: the accent's defining features — non-rhoticity, the trap–bath and lot–cloth vowel splits, a comparatively narrow and controlled pitch range, and precise weak-form reduction in function words — are the final layer of native-like control, since even grammatically perfect sentences will mark a speaker as non-native if nuclear stress placement or intonation contour is off. This is why sentence content throughout C2, but especially in batch 39, is chosen to maximise exposure to RP's characteristic phonostylistic patterns in context rather than in isolation.
